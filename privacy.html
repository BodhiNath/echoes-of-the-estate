Privacy Policy

Echoes of the Estate · Effective 17 October 2026

Summary

Echoes of the Estate does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the game stores

Your game progress (board state, chapter, coins, gems, room styles, and settings) is saved only on your device, in the browser or app storage of the game itself. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Network access

The game loads its files over HTTPS from GitHub Pages the first time it runs and when an update is available. Those requests are ordinary web requests and are subject to GitHub's privacy statement. The game sends no gameplay data, identifiers, or personal information with those requests.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and choose Erase progress, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the game stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
