## Full description
You inherited Halloway House: a decaying manor on the Washington coast, a box of cassette tapes from a great-aunt you barely knew, and a question nobody in town will answer.

MERGE TO RESTORE
Tap generators, drag matching items together, and build tools, keys, lamps, papers and timber up through five tiers. Merge five at once for a bonus. Deliver what each chapter needs and watch the estate come back to life.

A STORY THAT NEVER WAITS BEHIND A TIMER
Six chapters of family mystery. Once you have the items, the next scene is yours. No energy is ever spent on reading the story, solving a puzzle or restoring a room.

ROUTE THE WIRES
Two hand-built breaker-panel puzzles. Connect every terminal without crossing a wire. Wire every cell for bonus gems.

MAKE IT YOURS
Every room has fixtures with two styles. Crystal or ship lantern. Oak runner or bare walnut. Your Halloway House will not look like anyone else's.

HONEST BY DESIGN
